# Profit & Loss: how to read it

Open **Super Admin → Profit & Loss**. Select Consolidated, Café, Retail or a branch, set the inclusive IST dates, and apply filters. The previous period has the same number of days and ends immediately before the selected period. The CSV contains the selected and previous summaries, venture breakdown, expense categories and trends. Only the owner can open or export this report.

| Figure | Calculation and limitations |
| --- | --- |
| Gross billed sales | Original amounts billed after discounts, including recorded GST. A cancelled draft was never billed. Original cancelled or voided bills count in gross when a retained invoice or linked deletion reversal is available. |
| Cancellation and net recognized sales | The original invoice's business date receives one offset, regardless of whether the cancellation is visible in both billing and reversal records. Bills still recognized contribute their net billed amount less recorded GST. An actual refund does not reverse this revenue a second time. If historical tax or billing data are insufficient, recognized sales and profit are withheld. This is a report of the current state of invoices issued in the period, so a later correction can change the historical view; it is not an archived statutory period close. |
| Recorded COGS | New invoice lines store their checkout-time catalogue cost. For historical lines, a positive cost may be recovered from the saved taxable line value less its recorded gross profit. A zero inferred cost is treated as unknown because older checkout used zero for missing cost. The report never prices an old sale with today's catalogue price. Recorded cost is not a FIFO, weighted-average, or replacement-cost calculation. |
| Gross profit, margin and operating result | Shown only when all active sale lines have usable recorded costs and sales data is adequate. Gross profit is recognized sales less recorded COGS; gross margin is gross profit divided by recognized sales; operating result is gross profit less posted operating expenses. The operating result is based on the records entered and is **not verified net profit**. It does not estimate unrecorded bills, depreciation, income tax, financing items, or accounting adjustments. |
| Café and consolidated results | Menu items with recipe consumption enabled record ingredient quantities and catalogue costs at billing. A period containing Café lines without these snapshots is marked **Cost data incomplete**. Waste write-offs are tracked separately from invoice ingredient costs. A consolidated view requires cost coverage across the selected ventures. |
| Expenses and stock purchases | Posted operating expenses include credit expenses for the expense date; voided entries are excluded. Stock feeds, priced purchase receipts and separately recorded stock purchases are shown apart from COGS and operating expenses. A linked stock-feed expense is not counted as a second purchase. Purchase orders without receipt and supplier payments that settle a balance are not new costs. Historical purchase receipts lacking a saved receipt cost are flagged and omitted from the stock purchase subtotal. A standalone manual purchase without a link is treated as a separate recorded purchase; this does not infer an unrecorded relationship. |
| Receipts, refunds and Daily Closing | Cash, UPI, Card and other totals use actual recorded receipt dates, including customer repayments. Unpaid credit markers and corrected receipts do not count. Actual refunds appear on their own recorded date. Cash totals use the same rules as Daily Closing; opening and counted cash remain in Daily Closing. |
| Customer credit and supplier payables | Outstanding credit on selected invoices uses the current balance on those invoices, and can change with later payments. The venture or consolidated view separately shows current positive customer ledger balances across all dates, including opening balances and independent repayments. This ledger is shared across branches, so it does not give a reliable full branch balance or historic as-of balance. Purchase orders and cash outflows do not establish an unpaid supplier balance. The system has no supplier payables ledger, so the report does not estimate one. |
